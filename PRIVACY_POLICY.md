# Privacy Policy for HireHeat Extension

**Last Updated:** December 2024  
**Version:** 2.0

## Overview

HireHeat is a Chrome extension designed to help job seekers track competition levels for LinkedIn job postings. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our extension.

## Information We Collect

### Data Collected from LinkedIn

When you use HireHeat on LinkedIn job pages, we collect the following information:

- **Job Posting Data:**
  - Job ID numbers from LinkedIn URLs
  - Number of job applications (when available)
  - Number of job views (when available)
  - Job posting timestamps
  - Competition metrics and apply rates

- **Page Navigation Data:**
  - LinkedIn job page URLs you visit
  - Timing of page visits
  - Navigation patterns within LinkedIn job pages

### Settings and Preferences

We store your extension settings locally, including:

- Auto-refresh preferences
- Notification settings
- Competition threshold preferences (cold/warm/hot job classifications)
- Data retention preferences
- Extension activation status

### Usage Analytics

We collect basic usage information:

- Extension activation/deactivation events
- Error logs and debugging information
- Performance metrics for extension functionality

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Functions

- **Competition Tracking:** Calculate and display job competition levels based on application-to-view ratios
- **Trend Analysis:** Provide insights into job market competition over time
- **Notifications:** Alert you to low-competition job opportunities
- **Data Visualization:** Generate charts and statistics about job competition trends

### Extension Improvement

- Debug and fix technical issues
- Improve extension performance and reliability
- Enhance user experience based on usage patterns

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- **Primary Storage:** All job data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not upload your job tracking data to external servers
- **Data Retention:** Job data is retained based on your settings (default: 30 days)

### Data Security Measures

- Data is encrypted using Chrome's built-in storage security
- No transmission of personal data to third-party servers
- Local-only processing of all job competition calculations

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

### No Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:

- Share your job tracking data with third parties
- Sell your information to advertisers or data brokers
- Upload your LinkedIn activity to external servers
- Share your personal information with employers or recruiters

### LinkedIn Integration

Our extension:

- Only accesses publicly available job posting information on LinkedIn
- Does not access your LinkedIn profile, messages, or private information
- Does not post or modify content on your behalf
- Operates as a read-only tool for publicly visible job data

## Your Privacy Rights

### Data Control

- **View Your Data:** Access all stored job data through the extension's trends page
- **Delete Your Data:** Clear all stored data through the extension options
- **Export Your Data:** View and copy your job tracking statistics
- **Control Data Retention:** Set custom data retention periods (1-365 days)

### Extension Controls

- **Disable Tracking:** Turn off job tracking at any time
- **Pause Functionality:** Temporarily deactivate the extension
- **Uninstall:** Completely remove the extension and all associated data

## Permissions Explained

### Required Permissions

- **`activeTab`:** Allows the extension to read job information from the currently active LinkedIn tab
- **`storage`:** Enables local storage of job data and user preferences
- **`*://*.linkedin.com/*`:** Restricts extension functionality to LinkedIn domains only

### What We Don't Access

- Your LinkedIn login credentials
- Private messages or communications
- Personal profile information
- Contact lists or connections
- Information from other websites or tabs

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Automatic Deletion

- Job data older than your specified retention period is automatically deleted
- Default retention period is 30 days
- You can set retention periods from 1 to 365 days

### Manual Deletion

- Clear all data instantly through extension options
- Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data
- Individual job entries can be managed through the trends interface

## Children's Privacy

HireHeat is intended for job seekers and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from users under 13 years of age. The extension is designed for use by individuals seeking employment opportunities.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. When we make changes:

- We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Significant changes will be communicated through extension updates
- Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

## Technical Implementation

### Data Processing

- All data processing occurs locally in your browser
- No server-side processing or storage of personal data
- Calculations and analytics are performed using client-side JavaScript

### Error Handling

- Error logs are stored locally for debugging purposes
- No error data is transmitted to external servers
- Debug information can be cleared through extension options
